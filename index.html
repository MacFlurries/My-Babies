Bebe & Benji

Two tiny kittens showed up in a black backpack: Benji (Beng Beng Jagung Manis), the orange-and-white one, and Bebe (Beskuit Better), the tabby with the big serious eyes.

They were fed well, got chubby, and became terrible little rascals: biting, scratching, and pouncing on anything that moved. Benji scratched at the plastic tub. Bebe always knew where the litter box was.

Their mom, Lamine, the calico, was kind and smart, and always on dough mode.

They didn't die. I just lost them, because they had to move on to a foster home. This page is where they stay, running after my cursor forever.
